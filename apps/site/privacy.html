Privacy Policy

Controller

[RAZÃO SOCIAL], registered under [CPF/CNPJ], is the controller of personal data processed by Modus under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, LGPD). Contact: [E-MAIL DE CONTATO].

Data we process

Account: email and password (authentication by Supabase) and, if provided, display name and profile picture. Usage: the model, token counts and credits of each request, for billing and limits. Payments: identifiers, status and amounts of credit purchases reported by Mercado Pago. We never receive or store full card details.

Conversation content

Chats, files and projects stay on your computer. When you use a cloud model, the request passes through our server to the model provider only to generate the response. Local models run on your computer and do not send this content.

Purposes and legal bases

Providing the service and your account (performance of contract); processing payments and tax duties (legal obligation); preventing fraud and abuse (legitimate interest).

Sharing

Supabase (authentication and database), Mercado Pago (payments), the cloud model providers you choose, and GitHub (app update distribution). These processors may handle data outside Brazil under the safeguards of the LGPD.
